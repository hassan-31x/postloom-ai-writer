# Postloom design system
A creator writing beside a bright window needs a calm, legible workspace. Use a light neutral canvas with a restrained iris accent, inspired by the precision of Linear's product UI and the clarity of Notion's editor.
## Typography
Geist, locally bundled by the geist package. Regular through semibold; sentence case. Large marketing headings, compact product headings.
## Palette
Ink oklch(0.25 0.025 275), paper oklch(0.99 0.003 275), canvas oklch(0.975 0.004 275), iris oklch(0.52 0.20 280). Semantic green success, red error. Solid surfaces.
## Layout
Marketing hero with a substantial product preview; varied section rhythm. App sidebar and roomy editor with an adjacent live preview. Mobile navigation wraps and editor stacks.
## Components
Consistent rounded controls, thin borders, restrained elevation. Inline confirmations and errors. No invented metrics or dead controls. Phosphor icons.
## Motion
Short state transitions with ease out. A subtle marketing reveal only. Respect prefers-reduced-motion.
