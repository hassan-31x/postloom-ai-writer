# Product
## Register
product
## Users
Solo creators and small marketing teams creating social content in a daytime workspace. The user confirmed this audience and direction.
## Product Purpose
Turn rough ideas into editable social posts, maintain a personal voice, keep references, and save drafts for manual publishing.
## Brand Personality
Precise, composed, approachable. Premium and minimal, with the feel of a modern AI SaaS application.
## Anti-references
Generic AI landing pages, fabricated testimonials, neon gradients, decorative cards everywhere, and controls that imply unavailable capabilities.
## Design Principles
Show the actual writing workflow. Keep the draft under the user's control. Explain limits honestly. Make useful features easy to find. Use a consistent interface across all routes.
## Accessibility & Inclusion
Keyboard access, visible focus, semantic landmarks, descriptive labels, readable contrast and reduced motion support.
